## hubraum sparks innovation transfer and creates business opportunities for all sides

hubraum is Deutsche Telekom’s tech incubator and part of T-Labs. Through innovation programs and co-creation we bring startups, developers, ecosystem partners and Deutsche Telekom together.
T-Labs is the R&D division of Deutsche Telekom, focused on translating emerging technology trends into tangible outcomes for the group innovation portfolio.
Together, T-Labs and hubraum leverage synergies and serve as gateway for academia and startups to Deutsche Telekom. Our ambition is to explore and incubate emerging technologies for the future of network communication and infrastructure for a superior customer experience.
